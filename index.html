Who we are

MJT Consulting Engineers is a multi-disciplinary consulting company, operating professionally, with primary focus on Civil, structural, electrical and mechanical Engineering, Urban & Rural Development, Project Management and Construction Management and Supervision and Occupational Health and Safety.

It was formed in 2008 as a Close Corporation (CC). Since the government announced that all the CC companies have to be changed to (Pty) LTD, MJT Consulting Engineers changed in 2012. The company has 14 years in business. The company has a BEE rating of Level 1 and is a member of Water Institution of Southern Africa (WISA) and Consulting Engineering South Africa (CESA). MJT Consulting Engineers is ISO 9001-2015 Compliant.
