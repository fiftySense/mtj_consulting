Our story

MJT Consulting Engineers was formed in 2008 and later converted to a private company in 2012. We provide multi-disciplinary consulting engineering services for the built environment across South Africa.

Our focus is delivering practical, compliant solutions — from planning and investigations, through design and documentation, to supervision and close-out.
